# Client rehearsal sequence

Use RUNNING.md for complete setup and limitations.

1. Confirm Dollar Tree and source scope. Verify NVIDIA connectivity with your key.
2. Run intelligence. Confirm which collectors succeeded and which were empty/unavailable.
3. Results: inspect unique state/category labels, explanation cards, export units and accepted AI/fallback provenance.
4. Operational Impact Center: open Weather and Recall incidents. Inspect the mapping method, inventory assumptions, shared ATP and proposed actions. Review an exact lot versus an unresolved lot.
5. Operational Impact Center, What-if scenario tab: create a controlled event and open its saved detail. These scenarios are synthetic, isolated and cannot record live approvals.
6. Demand Planner: analyze a store, then change inputs and demonstrate that the old result is cleared.
7. Evidence Audit: export one run with its exact AI request/answer trace. Keep all screenshots associated with that same assessment.
8. Signal Search (hidden unless ENABLE_SIGNAL_SEARCH=true, because it needs an Apify account) is a separate, optional paid keyword lookup. Search Trends calls Apify and shows returned regions and related searches without a risk score or AI brief. It does not enter the main intelligence run.

Fresh installations do not contain seeded historical incidents. Use the What-if scenario tab in the Operational Impact Center for the controlled demonstration. Do not describe synthetic allocation as a live stock promise or scenario assumptions as measured client history.
